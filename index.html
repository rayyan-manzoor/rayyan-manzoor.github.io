JanaCafe

Bringing clean, quality matcha to everyday life.

JANA is a matcha beverage business I founded at 18 under Jana Ventures LLC. The brand is built around offering clean, high-quality matcha products that support wellness and daily balance. After extensive product development and branding work, JANA has grown into a physical location where customers can experience our drinks in person.

This project covers the full entrepreneurial journey — from establishing the LLC and developing the brand identity, to product testing, marketing strategy, and launching the storefront.

What I built

  • Registered Jana Ventures LLC and handled all business formation
  • Developed brand identity, packaging, and visual language
  • Managed product sourcing and quality testing
  • Built out marketing strategy and social presence
  • Launched and operated a physical storefront
